## Ghost Auto Clicker v1.1

### New: click wherever your mouse is
- No points needed: with an empty point list, **Start** (or F6) clicks at the mouse's current position.
- Uses your usual settings: clicks per second, button, hold time, random offset, breaks and the stop limit.
- If you start it with the Start button, it waits until you move the mouse off the app, so it doesn't click Stop
  straight away.
