## Ghost Auto Clicker v1.0

The first release: an auto clicker for Windows that can click in the background, on windows behind others,
on hidden browser tabs and on other virtual desktops.

### Features
- **Click points**: a list of spots, clicked one after another or all at the same time.
- **Window points** for any app: background mode (window can be behind others, your mouse stays free) or
  foreground mode (moves your real mouse).
- **Tab points**: launch an "automation browser" (Brave, Chrome or Edge) and click any of its tabs, even hidden ones.
- **Speed**: clicks per second, left/right button, hold time and a random offset.
- **Limits**: take a break after a set number of clicks, or stop automatically.
- **Reload pages**: after every N clicks on a point, refresh the page or open a URL, then wait for it to load.
- Edit several points at once.

### New look
- Two-column window: your points on the left (with a live click count for each), settings on the right in
  **Speed**, **Limits**, **Browser tabs**, **Windows** and **Hotkeys** tabs.
- Big Start/Stop button, total click counter and status bar.
- **Light and dark themes**, following your Windows setting (switch with the button at the top right).

### Custom hotkeys
- Defaults: **F6** start/stop, **F7** add a point under the mouse, **F8** remove the last point.
- Change them on the **Hotkeys** tab: any key, Ctrl/Shift/Alt combinations, or the middle and side mouse buttons.
  They're saved for next time.
