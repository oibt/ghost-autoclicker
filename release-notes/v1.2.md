## Ghost Auto Clicker v1.2

### New: hold or toggle
- The start/stop hotkey can now work two ways (choose on the **Hotkeys** tab):
  - **Toggle**: press to start, press again to stop (as before).
  - **Hold**: clicks only while you hold the key down, and stops when you let go.
- Works with any hotkey you've set, including combinations and mouse side buttons.

### Other
- The version number is now shown next to the app name.
- Releases renumbered: the earlier v7.0 and v7.1 are now v1.0 and v1.1.
